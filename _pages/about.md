I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Spanish National Research Council (CSIC) working at the 2D Foundry Research Group. I am located at Madrid's Materials Research Institute (ICMM) placed in the UAM Campus.

I am an expert in Scanning Probe Microscopy (SPM) design, building, testing, and characterization in varied environments (UHV, cryogenic). Importantly, my experience also includes the development of methods for the production of 2D materials.

I have a strong interest in AI and Quantum sensors for 2D materials and their heterostructures. Additionally, my entrepreneurial experience and IP skills have enabled me to contribute to the development of a scalable method for 2D materials production.

I am currently enjoying a CSIC Momentum Fellowship.
